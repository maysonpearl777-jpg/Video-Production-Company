Careers + Collaborations

Looking for film production jobs near you?

Sage & Frame brings together filmmakers, editors, producers, cinematographers, and emerging creatives who want to build meaningful careers without leaving the Midwest.

Join the Studio

Come make something with us.

Our Iowa City studio works on commercial campaigns, independent films, social content, documentaries, and wedding films. We look for curious people who care about visual storytelling and collaborative work.

Entry Level

Production Assistant

Assist our crew with equipment, set preparation, schedules, talent, and day-to-day production needs.

This role is designed for emerging filmmakers who want hands-on experience working on professional productions.

Part Time

Assistant Video Editor

Work with our post-production team to organize footage, prepare project files, edit short-form content, and assist with client projects.

Experience with professional editing software and a strong sense of visual storytelling are preferred.

Freelance

Cinematographer

Join our network of freelance cinematographers for commercial, documentary, wedding, and narrative productions throughout Iowa and neighboring states.

Applicants should have professional camera experience and a portfolio demonstrating strong composition and lighting skills.

Student Opportunities

Film production internships.

Our fictional internship program gives college students and emerging filmmakers experience with pre-production, production, editing, creative development, and client communication.

Interns work alongside our creative team, assist on professional shoots, and build practical experience that can be added to a portfolio or résumé.

Creative Careers

You shouldn't have to move to a coast to work in film.

Businesses, agencies, universities, artists, and independent filmmakers throughout the Midwest need talented creative professionals. Sage & Frame exists to help build that creative community here.
