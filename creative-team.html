Our Creative Team

Meet the Studio

Different perspectives. One shared vision.

Our team blends production experience with curiosity, collaboration, and a love for intentional storytelling.

Mara Ellis

Founder + Creative Director

Shapes the studio's creative direction and helps turn early ideas into clear, human-centered visual stories.

Theo Bennett

Director of Photography

Builds the visual language of each production through lighting, composition, camera movement, and atmosphere.

Lena Hart

Senior Producer

Keeps productions moving from concept to delivery while coordinating crews, schedules, locations, and clients.

Julian Cross

Editor + Colorist

Finds the rhythm of a story in post-production and gives each film a cohesive, polished visual finish.

Nora Vale

Production Designer

Develops sets, props, textures, and visual details that make each frame feel specific and lived-in.

Elias Monroe

Sound + Post Audio

Captures and shapes dialogue, ambience, and sound design to give each project a richer sense of place.

Camille Reed

Photographer + Content Producer

Owen Park

Motion Designer

Brings typography, graphics, titles, and animation into the studio's films and digital campaigns.

How We Work

Collaborative by design.

We believe the strongest creative work comes from listening closely, respecting every role on set, and giving good ideas room to grow. Our process is thoughtful, flexible, and grounded in the people behind each story.
